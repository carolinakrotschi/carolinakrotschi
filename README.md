### 🔬 Research & Scientific Software

**[pic-reflectometer-toolkit](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/pic-reflectometer-toolkit)**  
Optical frequency-domain reflectometry in Python: laser-sweep linearization using an auxiliary interferometer, µm-resolution reflectograms, and a simulator.

**[translation-stage-calibration](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/translation-stage-calibration)**  
Real-time fringe counting for calibrating motorized translation stages against a laser wavelength. Four detection schemes, quadrature homodyne direction detection, and a multithreaded GUI. Developed during my bachelor's thesis at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics in Nobel laureate Ferenc Krausz's research group.

**[interferometer-data-analysis](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/interferometer-data-analysis)**  
Laser characterization, including spectra, M² beam quality, intensity noise, and reproducible scientific figures.

**[bachelor-thesis](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/bachelor-thesis)**  
LaTeX source and PDF of my bachelor's thesis on interferometric translation-stage calibration.

### 🚀 Other Projects

**[NONLINEAR](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/nonlinear)**  
A science-communication project for young girls. Instead of following predefined experiments, girls develop their own ideas, test hypotheses, and experience what it feels like to be a scientist. The first prototype, *NONLINEAR Space Travel*, combines materials science, 3D design, and optics in an interactive mission kit for girls aged 11–14.

**[AIdgent — Industrial Agents Hackathon](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/Hackathonproject)**  
A voice-powered hospital navigation agent developed at the Industrial Agents Hackathon 2026 (**3rd place**). Combines an LLM-based interface with a deterministic spatial routing engine to locate staff, patients, and equipment and calculate routes through dynamic hospital environments. Built with Python, FastAPI, NetworkX, and JavaScript.
