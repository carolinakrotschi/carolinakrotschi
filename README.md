#### Projects

- **[pic-reflectometer-toolkit](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/pic-reflectometer-toolkit)** –
  Optical frequency-domain reflectometry in Python: laser-sweep linearization with an
  auxiliary interferometer, µm-resolution reflectograms, and a simulator.
- **[translation-stage-calibration](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/translation-stage-calibration)** –
  Real-time fringe counting that calibrates motorized stages against a laser wavelength.
  Four detection schemes, quadrature homodyne direction detection, multithreaded GUI.
  Bachelor thesis at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics in Ferenc Krausz (Nobel Prize 2023) group.
- **[interferometer-data-analysis](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/interferometer-data-analysis)** –
  Laser characterization (spectra, M², intensity noise) and reproducible thesis figures.
- **[bachelor-thesis](https://github.com/carolinakrotschi/bachelor-thesis)** – LaTeX source and PDF.
